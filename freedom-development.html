Freedom
Development

Freedom is a multifunctional community mall, which combines recreation, retail, business functions and life on one site.

The project is located in the largest park, on Mikhailovskaya Embankment, and is its infrastructural complement.

Main building
Shopping mall
Street retail
Commercial

Main Project Objective

Develop a corporate identity in a modern style, reflecting the modernity of the building, the main slogan is “Evolution of the city”. Develop a website and landing page showing the multifunctionality of the complex and its advantages.

Branding & Social Media

By the team was developed the corporate style and brand book “Freedom”. Created printed materials: booklets, business cards, diary, outdoor advertising.

Instagram Grid

The Instagram grid was designed to create a cohesive and recognizable brand presence on social media, combining consistent visuals, brand colors, and engaging content into a unified aesthetic.
